# Well Oiled – Strathcona Resources Equipment Optimization

## Project Overview

Well Oiled is a data analytics project focused on optimizing equipment selection and maintenance decisions for Strathcona Resources. By analyzing equipment failures, maintenance jobs, machine efficiency, and estimated maintenance costs, we aim to identify opportunities to improve operational performance, reduce expenses, and increase equipment reliability.

Our solution uses data-driven insights to identify equipment weaknesses, evaluate pump cost-to-performance ratios, and prioritize machines requiring optimization.

## Problem Statement

Which equipment specifications provide the best balance between high efficiency, low maintenance costs, and minimal failure rates?

Equipment failures and maintenance expenses can significantly impact operational efficiency. Identifying common failure patterns and evaluating equipment performance can help guide more informed equipment selection and maintenance decisions.

## Project Objectives

Our analysis focuses on four key areas:

1. **Equipment Failure Analysis**
   - Identify the most common equipment failure types.
   - Determine which components contribute most to overall failures.
   - Highlight opportunities to improve equipment reliability.

2. **Maintenance Job Analysis**
   - Identify equipment makes with the highest maintenance job counts.
   - Understand where maintenance resources are most frequently utilized.

3. **Machine Optimality Analysis**
   - Compare pump makes based on median efficiency and estimated maintenance costs.
   - Calculate cost-to-efficiency ratios to identify promising equipment specifications.
   - Rank equipment based on cost-to-performance.

4. **Priority Optimization Zones**
   - Identify machines with below-average efficiency and high maintenance costs.
   - Establish thresholds to prioritize equipment for further investigation and maintenance.

## Technology Stack

- **Databricks:** Data exploration and analysis.
- **SQL:** Data querying, aggregation, and calculations.
- **Power BI:** Data visualization and reporting.

## Key Findings

Our analysis identified several important findings:

- Rod-related failures account for approximately 76% of recorded equipment failures, making rods the largest contributor to equipment failures.
- Weatherford recorded the highest number of maintenance jobs, followed by PCM, LSI, and MANTL.
- Europump (PCM) demonstrated the strongest cost-to-efficiency ratio among the top three pump makes evaluated.
- Machines with efficiency below 69.2% and estimated maintenance costs above $22,900 were identified as priority optimization candidates.

## Solution & Expected Impact

Well Oiled provides a data-driven approach to equipment selection and maintenance prioritization.

By identifying common equipment weaknesses, evaluating cost-to-performance ratios, and highlighting machines requiring optimization, our solution helps support:

- Improved equipment reliability.
- Reduced unnecessary maintenance expenses.
- More efficient allocation of maintenance resources.
- Better-informed equipment selection decisions.
- Improved overall operational efficiency.
